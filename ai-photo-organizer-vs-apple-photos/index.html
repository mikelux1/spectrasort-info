SpectraSort vs Apple Photos.

These two aren't really rivals; they do different jobs on the same library. Apple Photos is where your photos live, with browsing, albums, Memories and storage. SpectraSort is a picking layer that sits on top, with ranking, culling, deduping and a learned taste profile. This page maps out where each one is the right tool.

What Apple Photos is built for

Apple Photos is a genuinely good library. It stores everything, syncs through iCloud, builds Memories, keeps albums, and since iOS 18 its search understands natural language. Its Utilities section can also merge exact and near-exact duplicate files.

What it doesn't do is decide for you. Search returns matches without ranking them. There's no quality score, no best-of-these-60, no sense of which frames you personally keep, and reviewing a trip still means opening photos one at a time.

They share one library, so use both

SpectraSort reads and writes the same photo library. Albums it makes appear in Apple Photos like any other album, its favorites are Apple Photos favorites, and its deletions go through the system dialog into Recently Deleted. Nothing forks and nothing gets exported.

In practice you live in Apple Photos and open SpectraSort when there's a decision to make: a trip to cull, duplicates to clear, an album to fill.

When Apple Photos alone is enough

If your library is small and you already cull it by hand, you don't need a picker. SpectraSort starts to make sense once the deciding becomes the bottleneck: thousands of photos, repeated moments, and no time to review them all.

FAQ

SpectraSort & Apple Photos.

Does SpectraSort replace Apple Photos?
No. It works on top of the same library. Apple Photos stays your browser and storage, and SpectraSort adds ranking, culling, dedupe stacks and a taste profile. The organizer guide covers how the two layers fit together.
Can Apple Photos rank photos by quality?
No. Its search returns matches and its Memories curate highlights, but there's no per-photo quality score, no best-first ordering, and no learning from what you personally keep.
Do SpectraSort's albums show up in Apple Photos?
Yes. Batch Process saves picks to normal photo albums, visible in Apple Photos immediately.
Is SpectraSort as private as Apple Photos?
Both process on-device. SpectraSort additionally has no account and no server in the path; its models never train on your photos. The architecture →
