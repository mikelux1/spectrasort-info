SpectraSort · Studio

Take the controls.

Studio is the deep end — where you decide exactly what "best" means and manage the profiles that carry your taste. Seven quality axes, a weight wheel that splits subject against quality, up to three subjects at once, and profiles you can save, switch, and bookmark. Most people never need it; when you want it, it's all here.

Quality focus

Seven axes, in your hands.

Every photo is scored across seven quality axes. Drag any axis by hand and the shape redraws live, showing exactly what your current setting rewards.

The technical side — sharpness, exposure, contrast, noise — anchors one half of the chart; the artistic side — composition, color, balance — the other. Shape it until it's exactly your eye, then save it as a profile.

  • Sharpness
  • Composition
  • Exposure
  • Color
  • Contrast
  • Balance
  • Noise
Weight wheel

Subject or quality — you set the split.

How much should the subject matter?

One wheel decides how much of the score comes from your subjects and how much from pure quality — the split reads out live as you turn it. At 82 / 18, photos of what you love win even a stop underexposed. Flip it, and craft takes over, whatever the photo is of.

Anywhere in between, both signals blend in exactly the proportion you set.

Subjects

Three subjects at once. Even a photo as a subject.

In the Toolbox you point at one thing at a time. Studio stacks up to three subjects into a single profile — dogs + beach + sunset — and weighs them together against your quality settings.

A subject doesn't have to be a word, either. Hand it a reference photo and its look joins the mix — blend up to three photos into one profile that finds more of that feeling.

Profiles

Save a look. Switch between them.

A profile bundles your subjects, your seven quality weights, and your split into one setting you can name, save, and reach for again — from any tool, any scene, any slice of the Timeline.

Your Taste
Learned from your picks, on-device. Bookmark it, pause or resume its learning, reset and rebuild — and in Studio, give it a name. How it learns →
Custom
Start turning any dial and you're in a custom profile: your subjects, your seven weights, your split. Save it under a name when it's right — City & Urban, Beach Trips, whatever it is.
Bookmarked
Everything you've saved, one tap apart — a beach-trip profile, a food profile, your taste. The active one rides along everywhere the app sorts.
