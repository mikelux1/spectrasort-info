Find the trip

It's already a Scene.

Open Discover and the trip is there, gathered by time and place — no album needed. Or tap Photos of a location and pin the city: every shot taken there, nothing else.

Photos from years ago count too. The pin finds the earlier trip you forgot you photographed.

Rank

The keepers rise before you swipe.

Best Shots puts the sharpest, best-composed frames on top. Your Taste, once it knows you, puts the ones you'd have picked on top. Either way the review starts with the winners, not with photo 1 of 1,200.

Lean the slider artistic for the sunsets and technical for the group shots.

Retakes

11 shots of the same sunset → one swipe.

Sunsets, group shots, the view from the hotel: every moment you photographed six times arrives as one stack with the sharpest flagged. Roll the wheel, keep one, move on.

Finish

Album made. Rest cleared.

Batch Process puts the picks in an album that shows up in Apple Photos — and in the share sheet, for the group chat. Keep the rest, or clear them through the system dialog into Recently Deleted.

The fine print

Scenes, the scores and the taste profile all run on the iPhone's Neural Engine. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account. More on how that works →

Where it won't help
  • iPhone only, iOS 18.5 or later. No iPad, Android or desktop version.
  • The very first index of a big library takes some time. After that it's automatic — new photos are indexed as they appear, so you don't wait again.
  • Place search by name uses Apple Maps and needs a connection. Scenes, the Timeline and the sort itself work offline.
  • Deleted photos go through iOS's own trash: they land in Recently Deleted, and the storage comes back the moment you empty that album there — or automatically after 30 days.

FAQ

Trip questions.

How long does it take to go through a trip's photos?
The sort itself takes a moment once the library is indexed — the first index runs at up to 100 photos a second on recent iPhones and happens once. Your swipes are the real time, and ranking plus stacking cut them down: the winners are on top and every retake costs one gesture.
What happens to the photos I don't pick?
Nothing, unless you say so. Keep is a bucket, not a delete. Anything you do clear goes through the system dialog into Recently Deleted, where it stays for 30 days.
Can I do this on the plane, offline?
Sorting, ranking and review run entirely on the phone, so yes. Searching for a place by name uses Apple Maps and needs a connection; opening the trip from Scenes or the Timeline does not.
Can I sort several trips at once?
In the Timeline, tick as many months as you like and sort them together. Or filter by a custom date range that spans the whole summer.
Does it upload my trip photos?
No. Scenes, the scores and the taste profile are computed and stored on your iPhone. No uploads, no account.
