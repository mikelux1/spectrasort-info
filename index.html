FAQ

Common questions.

Do my photos ever leave my phone?
No. Every step — the search, the scoring, the taste profile — runs on your iPhone's Neural Engine. There are no uploads, no account, and no SpectraSort server in the path.
Does SpectraSort use my photos to train AI?
No. Nothing is uploaded, so there is nothing to train on. Everything happens on your iPhone, and the maker never sees your photos.
How is this different from Apple Photos or Google Photos?
Those are built for browsing and storage. SpectraSort is built for picking — it ranks what it finds against what you asked for, or against what it has learned about your taste, then hands you a review flow to act on. And it's 100% local: nothing about your library leaves the phone.
How fast is it?
The first sort builds an on-device index — up to 100 photos a second on recent iPhones. Every sort after runs on that index and finishes near-instantly, even across thousands of photos.
What's free and what does Premium unlock?
Five sorts a week are free for everyone, forever — counted when the Review screen opens, reset Monday. Saving, sharing and deleting are free and unlimited. Premium is $19.99 once: unlimited sorts plus Studio (quality axes, the weight wheel, saved profiles). No subscription.
Does it free up storage?
It clears the photos you tell it to. Those photos go to Recently Deleted, and the storage comes back when you empty that album yourself or after 30 days. SpectraSort only ever touches your photo library, never the rest of your phone.
What is the Your Taste profile?
A personal profile that takes shape as you use the app, built from the photos you keep and the ones you let go — all on your device. Once it has learned enough it sorts by your own preferences. Shape it in Studio, or reset and rebuild it any time. It never leaves your phone.
I bought Premium in SpectraSort 2.x — do I keep it?
Yes — same purchase, carries over automatically. If it doesn't show on a new phone, tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
What devices does it run on?
iPhone with iOS 18.5 or later. All processing runs on Apple's Neural Engine; newer chips index and sort faster. No Apple Intelligence required and no region locks.
