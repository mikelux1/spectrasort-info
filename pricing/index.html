What counts

One sort = one Review screen.

A sort is counted when the Review screen opens — whichever tool started it, however many photos it holds. Five a week, reset Monday.

Saving, sharing and deleting your results are never counted and never limited. Neither is browsing Scenes, the Timeline, or the taste profile learning from what you keep.

FAQ

Pricing questions.

Is SpectraSort a subscription?
No. The free tier has no end date, and Premium is a single $19.99 purchase. Nothing renews.
Is there a free trial?
The free tier isn't a trial — it doesn't expire. Five sorts a week, every week, with every tool, filter and batch action included.
What counts as a sort?
A sort is counted when the Review screen opens, whichever tool started it and however many photos it holds. Five a week, reset Monday. Saving, sharing and deleting your results are never counted.
Why one-time instead of a subscription?
A photo picker isn't a service. Everything runs on your phone — there's no server, no account and no storage to pay for month after month, so there's nothing to pass on.
Do I need an account or a card to start?
No. Download it and sort. Premium is bought through the App Store with your Apple ID, like any app.
I bought Premium in SpectraSort 2.x — do I keep it?
Yes. Same purchase, carries over automatically. If it doesn't show on a new phone, tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
Does the price differ by country?
Apple sets the local equivalent of $19.99 in each storefront. The Mainland China edition, 光谱挑片, is ¥18 once on the same terms.
