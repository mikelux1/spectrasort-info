What an AI photo picker actually is

Three kinds of app get confused with each other:

  • Photo search finds matches. Ask for "beach" and you get every beach photo you've ever taken — 87 of them, unranked.
  • Photo cleaners find garbage: exact duplicates, screenshots, blur. Useful, but they only know what's bad.
  • A photo picker decides. It scores every photo — sharpness, exposure, composition, resemblance to what you asked for — and returns the set ranked, best first, with a way to act on the answer.

The problem was never finding your photos. It's deciding among them. That's the job SpectraSort is built for.

The part no other app does

It learns which photos you'd keep.

Most AI scores photos against a universal idea of "good": sharp, well-lit, centered. But your best photos aren't generically good — they're yours. The backlit one. The dog mid-shake. The imperfect one you'd print anyway.

SpectraSort builds a taste profile from every keep and reject you make during review. Once it has learned enough, "sort by Your Taste" starts producing the album you would have hand-picked. Pause it, reset it, or save several profiles — all of it stays on your phone.

FAQ

AI photo picker questions.

What is an AI photo picker?
An app that decides which of your photos are worth keeping, not just which ones match a search. It scores each photo — sharpness, exposure, composition, or resemblance to the photos you usually keep — and returns them ranked best-first, with a workflow to keep, share or clear the rest.
How is that different from photo search?
Search answers "which photos match?" — it can return 87 photos of a beach. A picker answers "which ones are the keepers?" — it ranks those 87 and hands you the best three. SpectraSort does both: every search comes back ranked. See picker vs culling for the full breakdown.
Does SpectraSort upload my photos to pick them?
No. The search, the scoring and the taste profile all run on your iPhone's Neural Engine. No uploads, no account, no SpectraSort server in the path.
Will it delete photos on its own?
No. Nothing is applied until you tap, and deletions always go through the system Photos confirmation dialog. Deleted photos sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days before the space is reclaimed.
How much does it cost?
Five sorts a week are free, forever — not a trial. Premium is $19.99 once for unlimited sorts plus Studio's full controls. No subscription.
