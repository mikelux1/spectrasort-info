The facts

What SpectraSort is, in one table.

SpectraSort is an on-device AI photo picker for iPhone that ranks your camera roll by quality and your own taste. It also culls, dedupes and cleans up. $19.99 once, no subscription.

NameSpectraSort
What it isAn on-device AI photo picker for iPhone. It ranks a camera roll by quality and by a taste profile learned from what the user keeps and rejects. It also culls, dedupes and cleans up.
PlatformiPhone, iOS 18.5 or later. No iPad, Mac, Android or web version.
LanguagesEnglish, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil). The Mainland China edition is 光谱挑片.
PriceFree download. Five actions a week are free forever; $19.99 once for unlimited actions and Studio. No subscription, no account, no credit card to start.
Privacy100% on-device. The search, scoring and taste profile run on the iPhone's Neural Engine. No photo uploads, no account, no SpectraSort server.
SpeedUp to 100 photos a second on recent iPhones. The index is built on the first sort, then kept current automatically.
ToolsSix one-tap tools: Your Taste, Best Shots, Find a Subject, Like This Photo, Dedupe, Photos of a location. Studio adds seven quality axes, up to three subjects and saved profiles.
First releasedJanuary 2026. Version 3.2 shipped November 2026.

New in 3.2

Five languages. Less paper.

Version 3.2 adds French, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil) to the whole interface, and to the App Store listing. It also adds a Documents & receipts switch to Filters → Exclude: screenshots, forms and photos of paper are held back before a sort, so the review is the photos the user meant to keep.

Sharing was fixed in the same release: Instagram, WeChat and Xiaohongshu now accept the photo, upright, for single and multi-photo shares. The full 3.2 notes →

The maker

Made by Mike Lu.

Mike Lu is the independent developer behind SpectraSort. He built the app around a question most photo tools skip: not “is this a good photo?”, but “would you keep it?” The app learns from the keep and reject decisions a person already makes, then ranks the library the same way.

SpectraSort first released in January 2026. It is made without a server, an account or a subscription — the app and the business model are both deliberately small.

Angles worth checking

Three stories in the app.

  • The anti-subscription utility. One $19.99 purchase, no subscription, no account, no server. A photo app that does not need a cloud.
  • On-device AI with no Apple Intelligence requirement. The scoring, search and taste profile run on the Neural Engine, offline, on iOS 18.5 and later.
  • Five languages at once. Version 3.2 localizes the whole interface and the App Store listing into French, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil).
What we will not claim
  • No “better than Apple Photos” claim. They are built for different jobs: Apple Photos browses and stores; SpectraSort picks and ranks.
  • No accuracy percentage for the documents classifier. It is a filter, not a promise.
  • No cloud, no account, no training on user photos — those are architecture facts, not marketing lines.
