How to choose a photo picker.

Every app in this category promises to find your best photos. Five questions separate the ones that actually decide from the ones that just match. Here's the checklist — judge SpectraSort with it too.

The five questions

  1. Does it rank, or just match? Search that returns 87 unranked beach photos hasn't picked anything. The whole job is the ordering.
  2. Whose taste is it using? A universal "good photo" model finds sharp, well-lit, centered. Your keepers aren't generic. The question is whether the app learns from your keeps and rejects.
  3. Where do your photos go? AI needs to look at every photo to judge it. On a server, that's your entire library uploaded. On-device means it never leaves the phone.
  4. Can you finish? Finding winners is half the job. Look for a review flow and a batch step — album, share, clear — or you'll be acting on the results by hand.
  5. What's the pricing model? Subscriptions charge you forever for a job that's mostly done after the first month. A one-time price fits how culling is actually used.

When SpectraSort isn't the pick

An honest chooser page should say so:

  • You're on Android or iPad. SpectraSort is iPhone-only (iOS 18.5+).
  • You want cloud backup. That's a storage job — Google Photos does storage; SpectraSort does picking. They coexist fine.
  • You only need exact duplicates merged. iOS's built-in Duplicates album already does that — here's where Apple Photos ends and SpectraSort begins.

If the job is deciding — which of these thousands are worth keeping, shown best-first, finished in one screen — that's the job SpectraSort was built for.

Choosing a picker.

What is the best photo picker for iPhone?
Judge any candidate by five questions: does it rank rather than just match, does it learn your personal taste, does it process photos on-device, does it include a finish workflow, and is the pricing one-time or forever. SpectraSort is built to answer yes to all five — but run the checklist on anything you're considering.
Are there free photo pickers?
SpectraSort's free tier is five full sorts a week, forever — not a trial — with saving, sharing and deleting unlimited. Premium ($19.99 once) removes the weekly cap and unlocks Studio.
Do photo picker apps upload my photos?
Many process photos in the cloud, which means uploading your library. SpectraSort runs entirely on the iPhone's Neural Engine — nothing is uploaded and there is no account. More on the private architecture →
What's the difference between a photo picker and a photo cleaner?
Cleaners find what's bad — duplicates, screenshots, blur. A picker decides what's best, ranked against quality and your taste, and gives you a workflow to act on it. SpectraSort includes the cleaning (Dedupe) as one tool of six.
