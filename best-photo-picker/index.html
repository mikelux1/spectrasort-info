How to choose a photo picker.

Every app in this category promises to find your best photos. Whether one delivers comes down to five questions. They're below, along with SpectraSort's answers, and you should hold anything else you're considering to the same list.

The five questions

  1. Does it rank, or just match? A search that returns 87 unranked beach photos hasn't picked anything for you. The ordering is the entire job.
  2. Whose taste is it using? A generic model finds sharp, well lit and centered. If your keepers don't fit that pattern, the app needs to learn from your keeps and rejects, and most don't.
  3. Where do your photos go? The AI has to look at every photo to judge it. If that happens on a server, your whole library gets uploaded. If it happens on the phone, nothing does.
  4. Can you finish the job in the app? Finding winners is half the work. Without a review flow and a batch step for albums, sharing and deleting, you end up applying the results by hand.
  5. What's the pricing model? A subscription charges you forever for a job that's mostly done in the first month. A one-time price fits the way people use these apps.

When SpectraSort isn't the pick

A few situations where it isn't:

  • You're on Android or iPad. SpectraSort is iPhone-only (iOS 18.5+).
  • You want cloud backup. That's a storage product's job. Google Photos does storage; SpectraSort doesn't, and the two coexist fine.
  • You only need exact duplicates merged. iOS's built-in Duplicates album already does that. This page covers where Apple Photos ends and SpectraSort begins.

If what you need is to get from thousands of photos down to the ones worth keeping, with the best shown first and the whole thing finished in one screen, that's what SpectraSort does.

Choosing a picker.

What is the best photo picker for iPhone?
There's no single answer, but there is a reliable checklist: does it rank rather than just match, does it learn your taste, does it stay on-device, can you finish the job in it, and is the price one-time. SpectraSort answers yes to all five. Run the same list on anything else you're considering.
Are there free photo pickers?
SpectraSort gives you five full sorts a week for free, permanently, and saving, sharing and deleting are never limited. Premium is $19.99 once and removes the weekly cap.
Do photo picker apps upload my photos?
Many do, since cloud processing means uploading your library. SpectraSort runs on the iPhone's Neural Engine instead; nothing is uploaded and there's no account. More on how that works →
What's the difference between a photo picker and a photo cleaner?
A cleaner finds what's bad: duplicates, screenshots, blur. A picker ranks what's good, against quality and your own taste, and gives you a way to act on it. SpectraSort includes the cleaning side as one of its six tools.
