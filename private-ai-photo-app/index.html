The questions to ask any AI photo app

  • Where does the AI run? “In the cloud” means your photos travel. “On-device” means they don't.
  • Is there an account? An account is a database row with your name on it. If there's no account, there's nothing to breach.
  • Are your photos used for training? If they're uploaded, read the terms twice.
  • Does the privacy claim match the App Privacy label? The label is part of the store listing; a marketing page can say anything.

SpectraSort's answers: on-device, no account, never trained on, and the label agrees.

How it's built

There's no server to trust.

SpectraSort indexes, searches and scores on the iPhone's Neural Engine, and your taste profile is built and stored on the device. No SpectraSort server ever handles your photos, so there's no privacy promise you have to take on faith.

The models ship pre-trained and don't learn from users' photos. The developer has no way to see your photo content, and Apple's App Privacy label on the listing says the same thing.

A side effect worth knowing: sorting doesn't need a connection, because the whole pipeline is local.

The one network feature

The app has optional anonymous usage analytics, using Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. What it sends are events like “sort started” and crash reports, never photo content and never locations. It's opt-in, defaults to off in the EU, UK and EEA, and can be switched off anywhere in Settings → Privacy.

That's the entire network footprint.

What else you get out of on-device

  • No account. Download it and sort.
  • No connection needed. The sorting pipeline runs locally, on a plane or in a dead zone.
  • No subscription. There are no server costs to pass on to you monthly. Five sorts a week are free, and $19.99 once removes the cap.
  • Speed. The index is built once, at up to 100 photos a second on recent iPhones, and every later sort runs against it.
Where it won't help
  • On-device also means the work happens on your chip, so newer iPhones index noticeably faster than older ones.
  • iPhone only, iOS 18.5 or later. No iPad, Android or desktop version.
  • The first index of a big library takes a while. It only happens once.
  • Deleted photos spend 30 days in iOS's Recently Deleted album, so the storage comes back later, not right away. SpectraSort doesn't show a “GB freed” number because it would be a guess.

FAQ

Privacy questions.

Do my photos ever leave my phone?
No. The search, the scoring and the taste profile all run on your iPhone's Neural Engine. There are no uploads, no account, and no SpectraSort server in the path.
Does SpectraSort use my photos to train AI?
No. Nothing is uploaded, so there is nothing to train on. The models ship pre-trained, and the maker never sees your photos.
Can the developer see my photos?
No. Processing is local and there's no account, so there's no path for it. Apple's App Privacy label on the listing backs this up.
Does it work without an internet connection?
Sorting works offline, since the pipeline is local. You need a connection to download the app or buy Premium.
Is there really no subscription?
Really. Five sorts a week are free forever; Premium is $19.99 exactly once, tied to your Apple account so it restores on a new phone.
